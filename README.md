---

### 🔭 Currently building
- 📱 **Lu** — A mobile platform for small-business growth and ecommerce in Namibia. Owners keep people, tasks, and stock in one record; buy and sell through a marketplace; see money move; apply for funding from lenders; and complete institutional forms with guidance. Shoppers buy locally, gig workers take on work (delivery first), and lenders review applications — same app, one business underneath. Lu started as a farming app (`lufarming` in the code). Those same needs turned out to apply to any small business.
- 🌞 Off-grid **solar-powered infrastructure** for internet and irrigation in rural Namibia.
- 📱 Mobile apps using **React Native, Expo, and NestJS**, built for ordinary phones and connections that drop out.
